# Walls Register to Structure

Standing rule: retaining walls (timber or ishigaki) and similar dressings are
**positioned and sized against the built structure they serve** — the deck edge, the
stair flank — with a **~1.5–2 stud standoff**, NOT placed at or sized for the
excavation/carve face.

## The rule

**Why:** the carve is temporary rough work (over-cut for margin); the built edge is the
datum the eye reads. Registering to the carve buried one ishigaki wall and
oversized/misplaced two timber walls in the NW1012 stairway build (2026-07-02) — the
owner moved them by hand and said "you keep burying walls in terrain."

**How to apply:** derive wall lines from the structure's frame (e.g. deck slab CFrame
± halfsize + standoff); after placing, rough-BACKFILL the void between the wall backs
and the cut face (additive fill, kept ~0.7 below the wall top) so the wall retains
something; the owner smooths. The full timber-retaining-wall recipe (level courses in
bays, proud posts, height cap) is on [[build-recipes]].
