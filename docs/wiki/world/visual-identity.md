# Visual Identity

The canonical marks and where they are law. **Check here before generating ANY art that
represents a game concept** — this page exists because a session invented literal
rock/paper/scissors sprites for a firework while the approved glyphs sat in the repo,
rendered on every throw button in front of the owner ("there are approved versions of
the RPS glyphs literally all over this experience").

## The throw glyphs — R / P / S

- **The marks are ABSTRACT, not literal**: R = ring (annulus), P = bar (round-capped),
  S = caret ∧. Not a rock, not a sheet of paper, not scissors. Owner-approved,
  gate-tuned, rendered from the PWA's own SVGs (`src/components/Symbols.tsx`) so web
  and Roblox share one identity.
- **Single source of truth: `roblox/src/shared/Glyphs.luau`** — image assets
  (`Glyphs.IMAGE`, two recolorable white layers per mark: core + outline), the day
  palette (gilt gold core / ink keyline), the night Neon meshes (`Glyphs.NIGHT`),
  seam-nudge data, and the render helpers everything uses.
- **Where they appear**: the HUD throw buttons, the drum/split-flap round display, the
  wall boards and tape, the chochin lanterns, the karesansui, night Neon glyphs, the
  PWA throughout — and, since 2026-09-06, dan's glyph act in the sky
  (`BurstStyles.TEXTURES.glyph_R/P/S` alias the cores for particle use).
- **Never redraw, restyle, or substitute them.** New surfaces consume `Glyphs.IMAGE`
  (or the aliases). If a surface genuinely needs a new treatment of the marks, that is
  an owner conversation first, and the result lands in `Glyphs.luau`, nowhere else.

## Other canonical art sources

- Firework particle sprites: `BurstStyles.TEXTURES` (dot/streak/salute flares),
  generated by `roblox/tools/textures/make_firework_sprites.py` / `make_salute_flares.py`.
- Dragon King boards: `roblox/src/shared/DragonKings.luau`.
- PWA character/tier assets: `server/src/constants/characters.ts` + `public/videos/`.

## The rule this page encodes

Before creating art for anything a player already sees elsewhere — a throw, a rank, a
character, a currency — search the repo and the live place for the existing asset
first. Generation comes AFTER the search proves absence, never before.
